Ordering Guide

How many cans should you order?

  • 50 guests: 50–75 cans
  • 100 guests: 100–150 cans
  • 200 guests: 200–300 cans
  • 500 guests: 500–750 cans

Final quantities depend on the number of guests, event duration and what other drinks will be available.

Why Nitecap?

  • Ready to serve: No prep required
  • Consistent: Every can delivers the same drink
  • Easy to stock: Simple to chill, transport and serve
  • Something different: Give your guests a fun alternative to the usual event drinks
  • Great for large events: Fast service without the need for a large cocktail-making team
